Your fuel distribution, down to the litre.

Datakö brings procurement, bonded stock, sales and regulatory flows together for fuel distributors — and makes the economic performance readable day by day.

See the breakdown

What the field shows

A tightly regulated business, run on estimates.

Fuel distribution follows a price schedule published every month. Between collection at the depot and the customer invoice, the value splits between customs, the Treasury, the funds and the distributor — and that split changes with the product, the sales regime and the mode of transport.

  1. The schedule changes monthly and is tracked in spreadsheets copied by hand.
  2. Nine sales regimes, each with its own breakdown.
  3. What is owed and what is due gets reconstructed after the fact, operation by operation.
  4. Bonded stock lives in the depot ledgers, the accounts live somewhere else.
  5. The real margin on an operation only appears at closing, sometimes later.
