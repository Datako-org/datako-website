Field reality

Complex operations, often poorly managed.

In emerging, fast-growing markets, unsuitable tools push teams toward fragmented communication and manual processes that erode profitability.

Without a central execution layer, every trip becomes a blind spot.

  1. Little visibility over trips and deliveries.
  2. Margins and profitability are hard to calculate per route.
  3. Dependence on unstructured personal WhatsApp exchanges.
  4. Approximate tracking of fuel, maintenance and driver advances.
  5. Scattered data that cannot be analyzed globally.

A platform built for execution.

Datakö Fleet replaces scattered spreadsheets with one operational foundation, keeping every operation connected to its cost and margin.
